The Challenge

Boda Boda Riders Face a Healthcare Gap

5 million boda boda riders power Kenya's transport economy — yet they are among the most underserved when it comes to affordable, accessible healthcare.

1k+

Boda boda riders in Kenya lack affordable healthcare access. The people who keep our cities moving are the least protected.

⚡
Long Wait Times
Hours lost at hospitals mean lost income for riders who depend on every trip to put food on the table.
💸
High Costs
Uninsured riders pay full price for consultations, drugs, and emergency care — with no financial safety net.
🚨
Accident Vulnerability
Boda riders face high accident rates with no fast-track emergency access or pre-registered medical records.
📋
No Medical Records
Each hospital visit starts from zero — no history, no continuity of care, no way to recall prior treatment.

One card. All your healthcare needs. Always ready.

The Solution

Introducing the HAMAT Smart Medical Card

A registered smart card that gives boda boda riders instant, affordable access to HAMAT's medical and pharmacy services — faster than walking in.

Digital & Physical Card with Embedded ID
Your card is both physical and digital — scan or tap for instant identification at any HAMAT facility, any time.
Linked to Your Health Profile & History
Every visit, prescription, and diagnosis stored securely. Doctors see your full story the moment you arrive.
NFC-Enabled for Instant Hospital Check-In
Tap your card at any HAMAT terminal — skip the queue, get fast-tracked for care immediately.
Emergency ID for First Responders
In an accident, first responders scan your card for immediate access to your critical medical details.
